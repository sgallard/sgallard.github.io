I am a Chilean PhD student in my 3rd year at **Université Côte d'Azur**. My research focus is on **optimizing newspaper production** using Artificial Intelligence and metaheuristics. 

I work closely with the French company **Demain un Autre Jour**, creators of the state-of-the-art newspaper production software *Melody*, through a **CIFRE contract**.

### Research Interests
- Combinatorial Optimization
- Artificial Intelligence
- Layouting Graphs
- Evolutionary Algorithms
